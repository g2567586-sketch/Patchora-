Patchora Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · Patchora for Android 1.0.0 · com.patchora2026.patchora

In short: Patchora keeps everything you save on your device and never sends it anywhere.

  • No account, no sign-in
  • No internet permission, no servers, no cloud sync
  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking

This policy describes how the Patchora Android app handles information. It reflects how the app actually works.

No account

Patchora does not require or offer an account, sign-in or registration.

Local storage only

Records, tags, labels, collections, links, recent searches and opening history are stored in a database on your device. Patchora excludes this data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so the system backup service does not copy it to Google or other servers.

No network access

Patchora does not request the internet permission and does not upload, sync or transmit your saved content to any server. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

Patchora requests no runtime permissions. Backup files are created and opened only through the Android file picker, which gives the app access to the single file you choose.

Backup files

A backup file is created only when you export one, at the location you choose. It contains everything you saved and is not encrypted. You are responsible for protecting exported files.

Copying and sharing

Content leaves the app only when you copy it to the clipboard or share it through the Android share sheet. Patchora shows the full text before a record is shared and never shares anything automatically.

Sensitive information

Records may contain credentials, tokens, internal hostnames or file paths. Patchora does not transmit them, but anyone with access to your unlocked device, your backups or your shared text could read them.

Children

Patchora is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Removing data

Use Settings › Clear all data to delete everything. Uninstalling the app also removes its locally stored data from the device. Exported backup files are not affected by either action.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently, this policy will be updated in that version before the change takes effect, and the effective date above will change.
