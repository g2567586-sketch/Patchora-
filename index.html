Tallyzen Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Tallyzen is designed to keep your information private. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Your data stays on your device

Counters, their values, settings and activity history are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

No account

Tallyzen does not have accounts, sign-in or profiles, and it does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information.

No servers

The app does not request internet access and does not transmit your counter data to any server. We do not collect, receive or sell any data.

No ads, analytics or tracking

Tallyzen does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

No sensitive permissions

Tallyzen does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, phone, messages or shared storage. Haptic feedback uses the standard system feedback and needs no permission.

Export and import

When you export, Tallyzen writes a JSON file to a location you choose with the system file picker. That file is under your control: anyone or any app you share it with can read your counters. When you import, the file you pick is read and checked locally on your device.

Android device backup

If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include Tallyzen's data in your device backup, managed by your device's backup service under its own terms. You control this in your system settings.

Deleting your data

Deleting a counter removes it and its activity history. Uninstalling Tallyzen or clearing its storage removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Tallyzen does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that change takes effect, and the effective date above will change.
